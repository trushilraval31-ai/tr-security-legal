TR Security — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TR Security handles information when used in Discord servers.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, TR Security may process Discord server IDs, channel IDs, server configuration settings, and security event information needed to operate its moderation and protection features.

2. Why We Use Information

Information may be used to save server settings, provide welcome features, detect spam or suspicious activity, and generate security alerts and logs.

3. Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in the bot's database or configuration storage. Retention depends on the information and the bot's configuration. Security logs should be deleted when they are no longer needed.

4. Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be processed by Discord and its services as necessary for the bot to function. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

5. Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect stored information and credentials. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your Choices

Server administrators can stop using the bot by removing it from their server. For questions about stored information or deletion requests, contact the TR Security maintainer through the support method provided with the bot.

7. Children's Privacy

TR Security is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Users must follow Discord's applicable age requirements and terms.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
